3. Exit rules at a glance

Options (priority)

  • Max size: 1 contract
  • SL: 20–50% of premium
  • TP: 30–100%+ of premium (aim 1:2)
  • Working pair: −20% / +40%
  • Spread: prefer ≤ 5%, reject > 10%
  • Broker: stop order first
  • TP monitored until OCO available
  • No new entries overnight; may hold with stop

Equity (fallback)

  • Max: 1 open position
  • Size: up to full buying power
  • TP: +25% of trade cost
  • SL: −20% of trade cost
  • Broker: stop order first
  • TP monitored until OCO available
  • Flatten before 16:00 ET (no overnight hold)
