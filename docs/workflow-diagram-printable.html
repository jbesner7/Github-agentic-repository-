Not RTHWrite skipped: outside_rth. No Robinhood calls. No scan. No buy.

2. A UniverseAll Robinhood watchlists. Drop crypto (BTC, ETH, XRP). Dedupe. Keep ADV ≥ 2,000,000.

Fail ADVIlliquid names never reach patterns or options.

3. B BiasDaily patterns first. 10-minute + hourly only on daily hits. Need a strict majority bullish or bearish.
→
No majoritySkip the name. Tie or mixed hits are not a trade.
↓
4. D StructureBullish → 1 long call. Bearish → 1 long put. DTE 0–7. ATM, else one strike OTM. Page the chain until ATM is in set.
→
No contractNo in-window expiry, or only far OTM after paging → skip.
↓
5. I Quote honestyGreeks only from Robinhood. |delta| 0.40–0.50. Spread prefer ≤5%, reject >10% of mid. No $0.10 override.
→
Fail quoteMissing Greek, wide book, or one-sided bid/ask → skip.
↓
6. E RiskSize = 1 contract. Cash = mid × 100. Must fit buying power. Working pair −20% stop / +40% target.
→
Fail cashPremium cash above buying power (example: SNOW $1,975 vs $1,500).
↓
7. Place authorityAlways review_* then place_* with the same params. regular_hours only. Max one new entry per run.
→
Blocked reviewBuying power, halt, or checks → do not place.

Any reject: journal the reason and stop. Pipeline code never places. Live place is F (this chat) or H (Automation) only.

Page 2 of 2 · Who may place, then what happens

Two authorities, one account, stop first

Do not run two place-capable Automations. Disable H to stop unsupervised buys.

F · This chat

  • May scan on request, including a Sunday test
  • Needs an explicit confirm of a specific order
  • Playbook RELEASED is not a blanket authorize-all
  • Off switch: do not confirm

H · Automation

  • Enabled as Agentic AI Bot
  • Clock check before any Robinhood call
  • No chat reply. Standing prompt is the permission
  • Off switch: Disable the Automation, or delete permissions file

After a fill

  • Place broker STOP only (−20% of fill premium)
  • Do not rest a live take-profit (no OCO in MCP)
  • Watch +40% on a later RTH run
  • If already in a position or working order: no new entry

Never

  • Crypto, shorts, credit spreads, multi-leg
  • Invented Greeks or weekend quotes as live marks
  • Account ending 5638, or any non-Agentic account
  • More than one open position

Write down

  • signals/* — last cycle JSON
  • journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md — skips and tickets
  • Mask accounts as ••••2907
  • H first Sunday fire: skipped, no RH calls
